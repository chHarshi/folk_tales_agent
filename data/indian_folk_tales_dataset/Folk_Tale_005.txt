Title: Folk Tale 005
Region: Northeast India
Language: English
Theme: compassion

## Folk Tale 005

In the verdant, mist-shrouded hills of Northeast India, where ancient trees reached for the sky and rivers carved their winding paths, lived a young man named Arem. He was a hunter of considerable skill, known for his keen eye and swift foot, but also for a spirit that often focused solely on the glory of the chase, rather than the quiet rhythms of the forest and its inhabitants beyond his prey. His village respected his prowess, but some elders whispered of a kindness yet to be fully awakened within him.

One fateful afternoon, Arem ventured deeper than usual, tracking a magnificent stag whose antlers promised great prestige. As he pressed on, the sky, which had been a clear, azure expanse, rapidly darkened. A furious monsoon storm erupted with startling suddenness, drenching the forest in sheets of rain and whipping winds, turning the familiar paths into a treacherous, muddy labyrinth. Lost and disoriented, Arem sought desperate shelter beneath a colossal banyan tree, hoping to wait out the deluge.

Huddled beneath its protective canopy, he discovered an old woman, frail and shivering, her ankle clearly twisted at an awkward angle. She was Lila, a reclusive elder known for her knowledge of herbs, now soaked to the bone and whimpering softly. Arem's first instinct was a surge of frustration; he was cold, lost, and now faced with the burden of another's distress. His thoughts were of his warm hut, his dry clothes, and the hunt he had lost, not of the shivering woman whose plight added to his already growing discomfort. He considered leaving her to find his own way, convinced she would only slow him down.

But as he met her pleading, watery gaze, a forgotten memory stirred – his grandmother’s gentle voice whispering about the sacred duty to aid those in dire need. The hunter's selfish resolve softened. With a sigh that released more than just breath, Arem knelt. He carefully bandaged her ankle with strips torn from his own loincloth, then shared his last piece of dried smoked fish, pressing it into her trembling hands. With immense effort, he hoisted her onto his back, sacrificing his own comfort for her safety, determined to get them both to shelter.

Through the lashing rain and deepening twilight, Arem carried Lila, his strong legs navigating the slick, uneven terrain. He no longer thought of the lost hunt or his personal discomfort, but only of reaching a safe haven for them both. After what felt like an eternity, he stumbled upon a small, derelict hunter’s lean-to, barely more than a frame of branches, but offering enough shelter from the worst of the storm. He managed to coax a small, sputtering fire to life, drying their clothes as best he could and keeping Lila warm through the long, arduous night.

With the first light of dawn, the storm broke, revealing a world washed clean and vibrant. Lila, her strength slowly returning, smiled warmly at Arem. "Young man," she rasped, "your kindness saved this old woman. I know paths through these woods that even the most seasoned hunters forget." She guided him not only through a swift, hidden shortcut back to his village, but also showed him rare medicinal plants growing abundantly nearby – remedies that would benefit his entire community for seasons to come. Arem returned to his village, not with a prized stag, but with the venerable Lila and a heart transformed, understanding that he had gained something far more valuable than any trophy.

From that day forward, Arem’s renown grew, not just as a hunter, but as a man of profound compassion. He understood that true strength lay not only in conquest, but in kindness, and that the greatest treasures were often found when one chose to lighten another's burden. And so, it is said in those hills that compassion, like a hidden spring, not only quenches the thirst of others but also enriches the very landscape of one's own life, revealing unexpected treasures and illuminating paths once unseen.
